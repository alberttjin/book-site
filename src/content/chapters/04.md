Back at the Defense Force HQ, an interesting character had joined Finn at the table. Rather elderly, the woman held a purple stone cane in her left hand. Her clothes however, did not bear the look of a quintessential grandma but rather someone much younger. Between them on the table, a game of some sort lay spread out with no indication of who was winning. 


As Jin and Yuna entered the building, the grandma instantly looked up, asking Yuna who the young man was.


“This is Jin, my new servant. I fished him out of the sea this morning” Yuna said matter of factly, with no indication of humor anywhere on her face.


The elderly grandma got up and approached Jin, eyes sparkling with intrigue. 


“What a handsome young man,” she said with an odd hunger in her eyes.


Jin took a step back. 


Reaching out her hands, she gripped his arm bicep, stroking it up and down before nestling it against her cheek. A shiver ran through Jin’s frozen body as he looked to Yuna for help. But Yuna merely shot him a mischievous smile. 


“This is Lilith. You’ve met Finn. They are my lieutenants.”


Jin did not care. Yuna’s exposition merely floated over his head as he stood preoccupied with the lustful grandma that had started examining his entire body. 


“Are you guys ready?” Yuna turned towards the two. 


“Are you sure?” Finn asked, eyebrows bent with slight concern.


“Yeah, I can handle it.”


“No I meant, showing him,” Finn signaled in Jin’s direction. 


Yuna looked at Jin, who was still fretting with anxiety attempting to shake an unusually strong Grandma off of him.


“It’s fine.” Yuna tapped at her sword while giving Jin a death stare. 


Finn nodded in agreement. 


Peeling Lilith off of Jin, Finn dragged her out into the courtyard while Yuna followed. They assumed formation.


Yuna stood in the middle, while the other two faced her. She drew one of her twin short swords from her back. A gold feather insignia on the handle of both of her swords glowed with intensity. 


Meanwhile, the other two held out objects of their own, each glowing with the same insignia. 


Lilith brandished her cane, slamming it on the ground with a power uncharacteristic of her frail, dehydrated frame.


Finn kicked his boots against the ground as they slowly enshrouded in a dark, shadowy smoke. 


Jin watched, shocked.


Artifacts? And three of them nonetheless?


Suddenly, a deep gong reverberated through the island. 


Gong.


Gong.


Gong.


A shrill chime followed.


Ding. Ding. Ding. 


The courtyard fell silent as everyone counted each ring.


… Twelve. Thirteen. Fourteen.


Their faces darkened, as they lowered their weapons. 


“Let’s go.” Yuna signaled towards the northern shore of the island. 


“Again?” Finn muttered. 


The group hastened in the same direction. Jin didn’t even have to ask. For in their world, this had become standard. A collision was coming. 


________________




Just before arriving on the northern front, Jin noticed a rather tall, well built man approaching from the opposite direction. His hair was slicked back and his strong brow ridge cast a terrifying shadow over his eyes. Refusing to run, he took exaggerated, heavy steps–each of his long strides seemingly teleporting him towards them.


As he approached, Jin felt the aura emanate off the man. Was this the strongest man on the island? Surely he was the real leader? Or maybe Yuna’s boyfriend? 


A mere 10 meters stood between them. Sweat trickled down Jin’s temple. 


Thud. 


Jin couldn’t believe his eyes. 


The man was on the ground.


He tripped. 


Quickly getting up, the man looked to the side, hiding the small tear trickling out of his right eye. A small scrape on his knee sought attention when the man desired it least.


Jin looked at the group, dumbfounded. But their expressions had not changed. 


“Report.” Yuna demanded. 


Standing up the man cleared his throat.


“T-the approaching island is W. We used to trade with them on friendly terms, until the <insert event> a few years ago. As you may remember, their political system is similar to ours. They have two leaders, each of which run half the island and half the army. With the way their island is positioned, we will clash with their Southern side. The leader will be Zaheen. End of report.”


“What’s the probability of full-blown war?” Yuna asked without hesitation.


The man looked hesitantly to the side. 


“It’s fine, Toren. Say it.”


“Very high, Commander. Zaheen believes to rebuild, they must go on the offensive.”


Yuna took a deep breath, clenched her teeth, and narrowed her eyes. 


“Thanks, Toren. Head back to your post.”


Toren’s cheeks flushed at the compliment.
