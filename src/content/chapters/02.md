Jin woke up an hour later. Above him, he noticed a rather low ceiling. The frayed, plywood slats left rather large holes that could only make one wonder what rodent families lived in the attic. The bed he lay rested on was quite firm, in a way that supported all the wrong parts of his body. Looking at his body, he saw that his wounds had been wrapped and tended to with fresh gauze made of linen. The white bandages around his chest had been soaked through with blood. 


The vile woman had likely caused him to reopen his wound. 


Groaning as he lifted himself out of bed, a young man appeared from around the corner. 


“You’re awake!” The man said with a bright smile. “My name is Finn, how do you feel?”


Jin narrowed his eyes, attempting to sharpen his blurred vision.


Before him stood a young man brimming with concern. His bright, shimmering eyes warmed Jin’s soul in a way that was reminiscent of the rising morning sun. Similarly, the man’s soft blonde hair glowed with the backlight of rays shining through the window. 


A tear slipped down Jin’s face. 


“What’s wrong?” Finn asked. “Are you still in pain?”


Jin smiled. 


“No, just a bad dream.” Yes, he thought. She was just a dream. A despicable, offensive dream. He had woken up in a nice hospital, on a nice friendly island, with this nice young doctor. 


“Oh, you’re up.”


Like a dark shadow swallowing the sun, his nightmare emerged from behind Finn. Her blazing amber eyes ironically sent a shiver down Jin’s spine. 


Shit. 


Without so much as a greeting, Yuna once again grabbed Jin by the shoulders.


“Now answer. X has long just collected taxes. They attacked? Why?”


Jin’s upper lip curled in disgust. 


“Now now, let’s let him rest, Leader,” Finn laughed softly, appeasing Yuna in a way he had done many times before. 


“Leader?” Jin asked reflexively.


“Leader of the North. The people and the army.” Finn responded matter of factly.


Seriously? Jin stared at Yuna, dumbfounded. She looked no older than he, likely in her 20’s. Her demeanor, the uncouth smile stretched widely across her face–-none of it befit a leader. 


Yuna raised her eyebrows, indicating her absolute impatience.


Jin sighed. “I have no idea. During the last collision, Prince X boarded the island. We thought he was collecting taxes as usual. Then, they ambushed us. The full force of their army. It didn’t seem like they were after one thing in particular. They destroyed crops. They stole artifacts. They crushed houses. Then they chased every high ranking executive, trying to kill us all. I barely got away.”


Not a complete lie.


Jin looked up. Yuna’s eyebrows furrowed as a twitch tugged at the left corner of her mouth. Her pupils had shrunk as if narrowing in on an enemy far beyond what the eye could see. 


Jin was unsurprised at this reaction. For a smaller island, it would be prudent to worry about the sudden aggressive nature of the largest island kingdom drifting on the ocean. But as he looked into Yuna’s eyes, he felt there was more to the story. 


Before he could ask, however, Yuna had slipped out of the building and Finn had followed.


Outside, Finn questioned Yuna.


“Can we trust him?”


Yuna crossed her arms in contemplation. 


“He could be what we needed.” She glanced harshly in Jin’s direction. “But we can’t trust him yet. He has some decent fighting skills. I’ll watch him”.
