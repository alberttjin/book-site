Surrounded by an ugly assortment of algae, pebbles and sharp pieces of driftwood, Yuna sat at the edge of the ocean, perched upon a rather large mossy rock. Beside her sat a large bucket, imprisoning many small trout, flopping for their lives. 


In her hand, she held a makeshift wooden fishing rod, but attached to the long translucent wire was a peculiar object. A sword. One of two, no longer strapped to her back but instead tied tightly near the top of the lure. Standing up, she wound back her rod with a routine expertise, before adeptly casting the line far into the sea. She closed her eyes, concentrating on controlling the power of her sword.


Suddenly, the rod was ripped from her hand and fell into the sea. Surprised, she promptly collected herself and swiftly shifted to her feet, diving off the rock and into the sea to retrieve her rod. 


Fighting against the current, she knew the moving island would abandon her in the sea if she took too long. However, Yuna paid no mind. Grinning, she raced through the water and before long was back at the shore, rod in hand.


Excitedly, she started to reel in her catch, only for the wood to snap in two.


Unwilling to give up, she grabbed the thin fishing wire with her bare hands and slowly but surely started to reel in her large catch. 


Blood dyed the wire red as it ripped through the flesh of her hand. 


But Yuna paid no mind. Bearing a barbaric grin, her tan muscles rippled in the dawning sunlight as she slowly but surely reeled in her morning prize. As the line reached the end and her catch became visible just below the seawater, something peculiar caught her attention.


A human face. She had reeled in a man.


As she scrambled to drag his body ashore, Yuna took note of the man’s condition. A large scar ran its way diagonally across his chest. From the darkish purple color and raised edges, she could tell that the wound happened quite recently. Smaller slash marks dotted his arms and face, some still actively bleeding. His silky white shirt, soaked with blood indicated a man of rather high status. The letter Y was branded into his right shoulder.


She pressed her ear to his chest.


He was alive.


Recalling the CPR training she had received from the island’s best doctor, she placed her hands on his chest. 


Up. Down. Up. Down. 


1 minute passed. Then 5. Then 10. She began to sweat. The man’s face remained pale and motionless. 


Remembering the other half of her CPR training, Yuna stopped. She leaned down, her silky wet hair cascading around the man’s face, shielding him from the sunlight. She brought her face closer to his till her lips were a mere inches away from his. 


The man opened his eyes.


A pause.


Startled, the man jumped up and scurried back. 


“Wh-what are you doing you pervert?” the man exclaimed, pointing at Yuna accusatorily. 


Suddenly, the man winced and folded, clutching his freshly formed chest scar. With a deep unsatisfied groan, he sat down on the beach and took stock of his surroundings before turning back around to look at Yuna. 


Yuna stared back, having not taken her eyes off of him for even a second. 


“A little help here?” His self-righteous tone matched the upper-class nature of his outfit.


Yuna started to walk slowly towards the strange man, not saying a word.


One step.


Then two.


“W-what do you want? You want my body after all, don’t you?” The man contorted to protect his private parts. 


Yuna put her hand on the hilt of her sword. 


Her third step became a lunge that closed the distance between them in an instant. Her blade carved toward his neck with killing intent. To her surprise, the man dodged down, barely avoiding a bad haircut. 


Jumping back, the man clutched his chest and his eyebrows narrowed.


“Seriously?” 


Without a word, Yuna closed the gap again, this time rotating into a roundhouse kick, aiming precisely for the man’s temples. Expectedly, the man dodged again. Having already adapted, however, Yuna used the momentum of the kick to spin into a downward slash of her blade in a seamless arc.


The strike landed.
The man staggered back before falling down, convinced he’d been sliced open.


Expecting to see an ocean of blood, the man looked down. 


What he saw was somehow worse.


He was naked.


“I KNEW IT. I KNEW YOU WERE A BARBARIC PERVERT. YOU WON’T GET AWAY WITH THIS WOMAN”, the man yelled, ears burning with embarrassment and anger. 


Yuna laughed as she sheathed her sword.


“Relax, I was just checking for weapons. I’m not interested in your pale little body. More importantly, identify yourself.”


The man took a pause to glance at his surroundings.


“Where am I?” the man asked, ignoring her request. 


“Island B, now identify yourself.” Yuna repeated, once again reaching for her sword.


Shit. Wrong destination.


“I’m Jin, a high ranking officer from Y island. We got attacked. I barely escaped–thought I was done for. Next thing I knew, I was here.”


Yuna studied the man. Apart from his exposed nature, the man seemed quite dignified. His lightly weaved pants and shirt were unlike anything made on her island. His soft, round eyes contrasted his sharp jawline and dark features as well as his multitude of scars and branded Y tattoo. His story seemed to check out, although she held her suspicions.


Yuna let go of her sword.


Jin watched, a smile threatening to shatter his poker face.


Good. She believed me.


“Y island is quite prosperous and large from what I remember. Who would dare to invade?” Yuna kept up her battering of questions.


A sharp pulse flared through his chest, forcing a grimace from his lips. He glanced up at Yuna, searching her face for even a hint of concern.


Nothing. 


There he sat, bleeding, cold and confused on the shore of a foreign island. And yet, all she had done was strip him naked and interrogate him like a criminal. A sharp sense of disdain started to build in his chest. 


Jin sighed. “X island,” he replied.


Yuna’s eyes widened with interest. She leapt towards the man, grabbing his firm shoulders.


“Really? Why? Why would X invade?” Yuna violently shook the man, disregarding his injuries.


A third pulse of pain shot through Jin’s body, this time significantly stronger than the others. He collapsed onto the beach, unconscious.
